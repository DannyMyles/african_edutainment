# Jua Crew: character briefs

*Working title. For: cultural consultants, the educator, and the character illustrator.*

**Jua Crew** is an original educational cartoon for children aged 5–8. Four children in a
real Nairobi neighbourhood, from families of different heritage, solve everyday mysteries
with science, maths and the stories their families tell. Each short episode teaches one
idea, in about a minute.

**How to read this document**
- **Settled:** personality, role in the stories and colour. These come from the show plan.
- **To decide with a consultant:** anything about heritage, meaning names, clothing,
  hairstyles, home details, food and language. Nothing in this document should be treated as
  a cultural fact until someone from that community has confirmed it. Where a question is
  open, it's marked **❓ Consultant**.
- **For the illustrator:** the deliverables and technical requirements are at the end.

---

## The world
- **Setting:** a real, specific Nairobi neighbourhood (❓ which one? It shapes the architecture,
  streets and background details). The kids' homes share a courtyard and a wall with a gecko.
- **Tone:** warm, funny and curious. Modern Nairobi as it is (schools, phones, markets,
  matatus), not a village postcard, and never poverty as the default.
- **Visual style:** bright, flat 2D colours, clear shapes and very readable faces. Characters
  must be recognisable from their silhouette and colour alone, even as small thumbnails.
- **Recurring places:** the shared courtyard, a classroom, Cucu Njeri's kitchen, the local
  market, and the sky (for science scenes).

---

## Zawadi (8), the inventor
- **Personality:** confident, energetic, a born builder. Makes gadgets out of scrap.
- **Weakness (drives the stories):** impatient. She skips steps, and her inventions break for
  the reason she skipped.
- **Teaches:** science and engineering. She models "try, fail, improve".
- **Relationships:** Kito's older cousin: protective, sometimes bossy with him. Friendly
  rivalry with Ada over who's right.
- **Voice:** quick and eager.
- **Colour:** orange `#e0782f`.
- **Signature item (proposal):** a tool pouch or belt with a screwdriver and tape.
- **Heritage:** Kenyan. ❓ Consultant: which community is her family from? Their name, hairstyle
  and what she wears at home versus school. Zawadi is Swahili for "gift"; please confirm the
  name suits the family you choose.

## Kito (6), the question-asker
- **Personality:** funny, curious and literal-minded. Always asks "But WHY?".
- **Weakness:** easily scared, jumps to conclusions (his shadow "ran away!").
- **Teaches:** he stands in for the viewer. His questions are the viewer's questions.
- **Relationships:** Zawadi's younger cousin. Adores Mjusi and believes everything the gecko
  says, at first.
- **Voice:** playful and full of wonder.
- **Colour:** blue `#3a86c8`.
- **Signature item (proposal):** an oversized cap, or a favourite toy he carries everywhere.
- **Heritage:** Kenyan, same family as Zawadi. ❓ Consultant: the name Kito, and family details
  consistent with Zawadi's.

## Ada (8), the storyteller
- **Personality:** a storyteller who loves words and languages, and collects new words in a
  notebook.
- **Weakness:** bossy. She wants to be right and to tell the story her way.
- **Teaches:** literacy, languages, and stories from her family's tradition (always attributed).
- **Relationships:** her family moved from Nigeria to Nairobi. Best friends with Tesfa.
- **Voice:** bright and lively.
- **Colour:** purple `#8e44ad`.
- **Signature item (proposal):** a word notebook and pencil.
- **Heritage:** Igbo family (Nigeria). ❓ Igbo consultant: the name Ada (and her full name),
  hairstyle, everyday clothing, which stories she may tell and how to attribute them, and
  any words she uses.

## Tesfa (9), the pattern-spotter
- **Personality:** calm and observant. Loves numbers and spots patterns everywhere.
- **Weakness:** shy. He often knows the answer but doesn't speak up, and learns to.
- **Teaches:** maths, patterns and logic.
- **Relationships:** the oldest of the four, the quiet one. Best friends with Ada.
- **Voice:** calm, gentle and soft-spoken.
- **Colour:** green `#2e8b57`.
- **Signature item (proposal):** a small abacus, or a tape measure.
- **Heritage:** Ethiopian-Kenyan family. ❓ Consultant: the name Tesfa, family background, which
  Ethiopian community, the languages spoken at home, and appearance details.

## Cucu Njeri, the grandmother and keeper of stories
- **Personality:** warm and witty. Hilariously bad with new technology.
- **Role:** introduces the "story time" segments and **always says where a story comes
  from**. Gives values and history the human side.
- **Relationships:** Zawadi and Kito's grandmother. The whole crew treats her kitchen as home.
- **Voice:** warm and unhurried.
- **Colour:** brown `#b5651d`.
- **Heritage:** Kikuyu (from the show plan). ❓ Kikuyu consultant: is the name right for a
  grandmother of this age, what does "Cucu" convey, her everyday clothing (not ceremonial
  dress for everyday scenes), her home, and which stories she may tell.

## Mjusi, the gecko who is always wrong
- **Personality:** over-confident, dramatic, very sure of himself, and usually wrong.
- **Role (important for teaching):** he says out loud the **common wrong idea** about each
  topic, so the kids, and the viewer, can test it and correct it. He must be funny, never
  mean, and happy to learn.
- **Relationships:** lives on the courtyard wall. Kito's biggest fan.
- **Voice:** cheeky and comic.
- **Colour:** olive green `#6b8e23`.
- **Look (proposal):** a small house gecko, the kind children see on walls at home, with big
  expressive eyes. ❓ Educator: is the species right for Nairobi homes? Mjusi is the Swahili
  word for lizard; please confirm the usage.

## The Narrator
- Voice only for now; opens and closes episodes and asks the viewer the final question. No
  artwork needed yet.

---

## For the illustrator

**Deliverables per character** (the Narrator needs none):
1. **Model sheet:** front, ¾, side and back views, with colour codes.
2. **Expressions:** 8–12 head close-ups.
3. **Cast height lineup:** all six characters side by side, to scale.
4. **Poses, as separate transparent PNGs:**
   `default` · `happy` · `surprised` · `thinking` · `explaining` · `excited` · `curious` · `proud` · `singing`
   - full body, facing slightly towards the camera,
   - the same scale for every character (the height lineup is the reference),
   - at least **1500 px tall**, transparent background, no cast shadow,
   - named exactly like this: `Kito/surprised.png`, `Zawadi/default.png`, …
5. **Source files:** layered (PSD, AI, or Krita), with **eyes, mouth, arms and head on
   separate layers**. This lets us rig the characters for animation later.

**Ground rules**
- Original designs only. No resemblance to existing cartoon characters.
- Heritage details follow the consultants' answers above. Please ask rather than guess.
- AI tools may be used for early exploration, but **the final artwork is drawn by you**.
- **Ownership:** the contract transfers full copyright in all delivered artwork to the
  channel owner (work-for-hire), including the right to modify and animate it.

**Suggested order:** first the height lineup and one character (Kito, the most-used) for
feedback, then the rest.
